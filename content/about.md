Hi, I'm Tejas Kale and welcome to my blog! I'm a data scientist currently residing in Berlin, Germany. I have over 11 years of experience building machine learning systems across different industries. Regardless, I learn something new every day and this blog is an attempt to document them. My current interests include usage and applications of LLMs, economic effects of LLMs on work and society, demand forecasting (especially labour demand in the context of retail supermarkets), and statistical experimentation.

As the blog is just getting started, I do not know how exactly it will shape up in the upcoming weeks. For now, the intention is to simply write consistently. Feel free to peruse through the posts and I always appreciate feedback and suggestions.
