Star Star in the second week of January 201, one star star. More than 10,000 businessmen from 100 countries descended on Gandhi Nagar for the fifth installment of the Biennial Summit, branded as vibrant Gourgeat. A marathon matchmaking ceremony of investment pledges and signings intended to bring business to Gosarot and not incidentally to make headlines while doing so. The 2011 Mela did not disappoint on either count. By the time it was all over, the businessman had promised investments in access of $450 billion, the largest ever sum for a single event in an emerging economy. And the media had a bludgingly trumpeted both the jaw-dropping figure and the unending chorus of corporate titans paying tribute to Gujarat and its chief minister.
